# Industry coverage: summary

The book is set in **one industry, hospitality** (a hotel front office). It adds **short examples from IT, banking, retail and healthcare** in most lessons. Nothing else is covered beyond passing mentions.

Source: the current `book-data.json` (all 196 units), audited on 3 October 2026. Full evidence is in [INDUSTRY-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md](INDUSTRY-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md) and [INDUSTRY-COVERAGE-MATRIX.csv](INDUSTRY-COVERAGE-MATRIX.csv). No book content was changed.

## The proposed marketing statement

> "Career English covers English for hospitality, healthcare, IT, banking, retail, BPO/call centres, education, aviation, tourism, sports and other professional sectors."

**Not supported as written.**
- Only hospitality is covered.
- Healthcare, IT, banking and retail appear as short examples only.
- BPO/call centres, aviation, sports, education and tourism are absent or too thin to claim.

**Defensible replacement:**

> "Career English teaches the English of everyday professional life — through one continuous workplace story set in a hotel, from a new employee's first day to leading a team — with short examples from banking, retail, IT and healthcare in most lessons."

## Cover

- **Front cover:** make no list of sectors (option B, or D). The current subtitle, "English for Your Professional Journey", is accurate. Keep it.
- **Back cover or blurb:** name sectors only as examples (option C): "set in a hotel workplace, with examples from banking, retail, IT and healthcare."
- **Never** list BPO, call centres, aviation, tourism, education or sports. Never say "covers" for any sector except hospitality.
