## DECISION 1 — LINEN CONTRACT

Level 4 Module 1 signs the Bengal Textile linen contract for **two years at a fixed rate (2027–2028)**. Later passages treat it as an **annual renewal one year later**.

| # | Location | Current wording | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L4 1.1, p. 617 | "raising prices for the new contract year" | Annual, before the deal (starting point) |
| 2 | L4 1.3, p. 629 | "sign for a longer term — say, two years" / "a fixed rate for the full two years" | Term changes to two years |
| 3 | L4 1.5, p. 640 | "8% increase … fixed for a two-year term … review limited to delivery costs" | **Two years, rate fixed** |
| 4 | L4 1.5, p. 641 | Written confirmation: "Contract Terms — 2027–2028 … two-year term" | **Two years** |
| 5 | L4 1.5, p. 642 | "a two-year linen supply contract closes…" | Two years |
| 6 | L4 8.2, p. 819 | Mr. Mostafa "returns a year later for the contract's renewal … revisiting … the rate" | **Annual** |
| 7 | L4 Capstone, p. 848 | "the annual renewal of the linen supply contract Arif first negotiated a year ago" | **Annual** |
| 8 | L4 Capstone, p. 848 | "most suppliers move toward a 15% adjustment" / Arif: "closer to a 5 to 6% adjustment" | Rate reopened after one year |
| 9 | L4 Capstone, p. 848 | "a two-year term instead of renewing year to year" | **Year-to-year** |
| 10 | L4 Capstone, p. 849 | "8% increase on this year's per-unit rate, fixed for a two-year term" | A new two-year deal after one year |
| 11 | L4 Capstone, p. 849 | "Good doing business with you again this year" | Annual |
| 12 | L4 Capstone rubric, p. 857 | "the vendor relationship's first year" | One year of history |

**Option A — Two-year contract (2027–2028) is canonical**
- Already consistent: #1–5.
- To correct: #6–11, and #12 if the timing changes. That is about 6–7 passages, all in L4 8.2 (one industry example) and the L4 capstone, Step 1.
- Typical fix: the capstone call happens when the two-year term ends, or Mr. Mostafa asks to reopen early and Arif holds him to the fixed term.
- Effects: no answer keys, codes or index entries change. The Module 1 teaching example stays exactly as it is.

**Option B — Annual renewal is canonical**
- Already consistent: #1, #6–12.
- To correct: Module 1 Lessons 1.3–1.5, which build the lesson around the two-year trade. About 40 sentences mention the term or the fixed rate: 37 in the lessons and 4 in their answer keys.
- These include the model dialogues, the PAT-0112 example, the MIS-0141 example, the 12-month review clause in 1.4, the close and written confirmation in 1.5, and vocabulary examples such as V-0617 ("If we can agree on a two-year term…").
- Effects: the lesson's model negotiation and four answer keys change. No codes or index entries change.

**AUTHOR DECISION: A** (two-year contract), applied.
