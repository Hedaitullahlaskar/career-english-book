## Book-wide decisions

- Lesson references use the book's numbering: "Lesson 1.2" = Module 1, Lesson 2 of the current level. Internal codes (L01, L02, Chapter 2 Part C, curriculum map) are removed.
- Answer keys give the answer first, then the reason. "Open-ended — check against the lesson's model language" is replaced with a sample answer plus success criteria.
- Audio does not exist yet: "Listen to the dialogue" becomes "Read the dialogue again (or listen to a partner or teacher read it aloud)".

- Missing Grammar-in-Context cards (the "See for the full card" / "— see." references) are written out in place.
- Dashes: " -- " becomes a spaced em dash; "->" becomes "→" (global rules).
