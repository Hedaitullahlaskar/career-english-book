## 1. Context
Your vegetable stall. Dev's potatoes cost ₹40 and onions cost ₹20: ₹60 in total. This stall accepts cash and UPI, but not cards.
- You are: Vegetable Seller; you own and run the stall alone.
- People: Dev (Customer, external)
- Goal: State the total, discuss the available payment methods, and confirm change or a known payment result.
- Note: Use only the prices and payment facts given in each practice.
- Note: Do not say a UPI payment is received unless the practice says it is confirmed.
- Note: Give no financial, regulatory or payment-system advice.

## 2. Notice
**Dev:** What is the total?
**You:** That is ₹60, please.  _CFN-0089_
**Dev:** Can I pay cash?
**You:** Yes. Cash or UPI is fine.  _CFN-0089_
**Dev:** Here is ₹100.
**You:** Here is your ₹40 change.  _CFN-0089_
**Dev:** Can I have a receipt?
**You:** Here is your receipt.  _CFN-0089_

## 3. Understand
- **That is ₹60, please.** (CFN-0089) State the total.
- **Cash or UPI is fine.** (CFN-0089) Say which payment methods are available.
- **Here is your ₹40 change.** (CFN-0089) Say the change you return.
- **Here is your receipt.** (CFN-0089) Give the payment record.
- cash — Money in notes or coins. _Can I pay cash?_ [V-VEGM-0011]
- change — Money returned when someone pays more than the total. _Here is your ₹40 change._ [V-VEGM-0012]
- receipt — A written record of a payment. _Here is your receipt._ [V-VEGM-0013]
- total — The full amount to pay. _The total is ₹60._ [V-VEGM-0014]
- UPI — A digital payment method used in India. _Cash or UPI is fine._ [V-VEGM-0015]

## 4. Guided practice
**GP1 (short-answer)** Say the change.
  - The total is ₹60. The customer gives you ₹100.
**GP2 (choose-response)** Use the payment fact given.
  - The UPI payment of ₹60 is confirmed as received. Choose your reply. (Your ₹60 payment is received. Thank you. / Here is your ₹40 change.)
**GP3 (choose-response)** State the available methods.
  - The customer asks, 'Can I pay by card?' Your stall accepts cash and UPI only. (Sorry, cash or UPI only. / Yes, all cards are fine.)

## 5. Role practice
Work in pairs. State the total and payment methods. Take the customer's cash, say the change and give a receipt. Then change roles.
- **Vegetable Seller**: Complete payment for the vegetables. The total is ₹75. You accept cash and UPI. For this practice, the customer gives you ₹100 cash. You have ₹25 change and a receipt.
- **Dev, customer**: Pay for the vegetables and get a receipt. Ask the total and whether cash is accepted. Give ₹100 cash. Ask for a receipt.
