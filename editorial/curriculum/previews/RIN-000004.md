## 1. Context
A dealership showroom. Meera has a written quotation for her selected vehicle version. It lists optional additions separately; their amounts still need confirmation.
- You are: Car Sales Executive responsible for explaining the quotation; price changes require the Sales Manager's confirmation.
- People: Meera (Customer, external); Farhan (Sales Manager, senior)
- Goal: Explain which quoted figure applies to the selected version and separate it from additions and an unconfirmed final total.
- Note: Refer to the written quotation without inventing numerical prices or charges.
- Note: Do not promise a price change, approval, finance terms or a final total awaiting confirmation.
- Note: Give no financial or regulatory advice.

## 2. Notice
**Meera:** Is this the price for the version I chose?
**You:** Yes. The figure beside your selected version is its quoted price.  _CFN-0085_
**Meera:** Does it include the optional additions below?
**You:** Those additions are listed separately. Their amounts still need confirmation.  _CFN-0085_
**Meera:** So I cannot use this figure as the total with additions?
**You:** That's right. It is the quoted version price, not a confirmed total with additions.  _CFN-0085_
**Meera:** Could the price change if I choose another version?
**You:** Another version needs its own quotation. I cannot confirm its price from this document.  _CFN-0085_
**Meera:** Please ask about the additions before I decide.
**You:** I'll ask Farhan to confirm their amounts before giving you a total.  _CFN-0085_

## 3. Understand
- **The figure beside your selected version is its quoted price.** (CFN-0085) Link a quoted amount to the exact version.
- **Those additions are listed separately.** (CFN-0085) Explain the quotation's scope.
- **Their amounts still need confirmation.** (CFN-0085) Keep unconfirmed charges separate from confirmed information.
- quotation — A document stating a proposed price and what it covers. _Let's look at the quotation for your selected version._ [V-CARD-0001]
- quoted price — The price stated in a quotation. _This figure is the quoted price for that version._ [V-CARD-0002]
- optional addition — An extra item the customer can choose. _The optional additions are listed separately._ [V-CARD-0003]
- included — Part of the stated price or package. _Is this item included in the quoted price?_ [V-CARD-0004]
- separately — Apart from another item or amount. _The additions are listed separately._ [V-CARD-0005]
- final total — The complete amount after all chosen items are confirmed. _The final total with additions is not confirmed._ [V-CARD-0006]
