## 1. Context
Your vegetable stall. Potatoes are sold by weight. Onions are also available as individual small or large ones.
- You are: Vegetable Seller; you own and run the stall alone.
- People: Sana (Customer, external)
- Goal: Ask how much the customer wants and confirm weight, number and size.
- Note: Use only the vegetables and amounts given in each practice.
- Note: Check unclear amounts before confirming the order.
- Note: Make no health, pesticide or certification claims.

## 2. Notice
**Sana:** Potatoes, please.
**You:** How much do you need?  _CFN-0086_
**Sana:** Half a kilo.
**You:** These weigh half a kilo.  _CFN-0086_
**Sana:** And two onions, please.
**You:** Two small ones or two large ones?  _CFN-0086_
**Sana:** Two large ones.
**You:** Half a kilo and two large onions.  _CFN-0086_
**Sana:** Yes, that's right.
**You:** Two large onions, as you asked.  _CFN-0086_

## 3. Understand
- **How much do you need?** (CFN-0086) Ask about weight.
- **How many do you want?** (CFN-0086) Ask about number.
- **These weigh half a kilo.** (CFN-0086) Say the weight.
- **Two small ones or two large ones?** (CFN-0086) Check the size.
- potato — A vegetable often sold by weight. _Half a kilo of potatoes, please._ [V-VEGM-0006]
- onion — A round vegetable with layers. _Two large onions, please._ [V-VEGM-0007]
- kilo — A short word for kilogram; a unit of weight. _One kilo of potatoes, please._ [V-VEGM-0008]
- half — One of two equal parts. _Half a kilo, please._ [V-VEGM-0009]
- weigh — To have a particular weight. _These potatoes weigh half a kilo._ [V-VEGM-0010]

## 4. Guided practice
**GP1 (choose-response)** Choose the question about weight.
  - A customer wants potatoes. You need the weight. (How much do you need? / How many large onions?)
**GP2 (gap-fill)** Ask about number.
  - How ___ onions do you want?
**GP3 (short-answer)** Confirm the order.
  - The customer wants half a kilo of potatoes and two large onions. Say both amounts.

## 5. Role practice
Work in pairs. Ask about weight, number and size. Repeat the complete order. The customer checks it. Then change roles.
- **Vegetable Seller**: Find out and confirm the order. You have potatoes and small or large onions. Ask for any missing amount or size.
- **Sana, customer**: Get the amounts you want. Start with 'Potatoes and onions, please.' You want half a kilo of potatoes and two large onions. Give the amounts when asked.

## 6. Performance task
A customer says, 'Potatoes and onions, please.' Ask for the missing details. Your partner wants one kilo of potatoes and three small onions. Confirm the complete order without reading the model.
- Ask for the potato weight.
- Ask for the number of onions.
- Check the onion size.
- Confirm one kilo of potatoes and three small onions.
