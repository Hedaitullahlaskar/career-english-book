## 1. Context
A dealership showroom. Kunal is comparing two versions of a model. The comparison sheet shows more boot space in the larger version. He wants a test-drive appointment; availability has not been checked.
- You are: Car Sales Executive coordinating the customer's appointment request.
- People: Kunal (Customer, external)
- Goal: Compare the stated space feature, ask permission to send an appointment request, and clarify the requested time, proposed meeting point and next contact.
- Note: Communication only: exclude CFN-0108.
- Note: No driving, road-safety, vehicle-operation or safety-training instructions; no regulatory or safety advice.
- Note: Refer to the dealership's test-drive policy generically; do not invent policy requirements.
- Note: Do not promise vehicle availability or an appointment before confirmation.

## 2. Notice
**Kunal:** Which version has more space for our luggage?
**You:** The comparison sheet shows more boot space in the larger version.  _CFN-0091_
**Kunal:** I'd like to arrange a test drive for that version.
**You:** May I send an appointment request for the larger version?  _CFN-0097_
**Kunal:** Yes, please. Friday at three would suit me.
**You:** May I include Friday at three as your preferred time?  _CFN-0097_
**Kunal:** Yes. Where would we meet?
**You:** May I suggest showroom reception as the meeting point, subject to appointment confirmation?  _CFN-0097_
**Kunal:** That works. Please email me when you know.
**You:** May I email you the confirmed details after checking availability under the dealership's test-drive policy?  _CFN-0097_
**Kunal:** Yes. I'll wait for that email before making plans.

## 3. Understand
- **The comparison sheet shows more boot space in the larger version.** (CFN-0091) Base a comparison on the supplied product information.
- **May I send an appointment request for the larger version?** (CFN-0097) Ask permission and wait for the customer's answer.
- **May I include Friday at three as your preferred time?** (CFN-0097) A preferred time is a request, not a confirmed booking.
- version — One form of a vehicle model with particular features. _Which version would you like to try?_ [V-CARD-0007]
- boot space — Room for luggage in the back storage area of a car. _The larger version has more boot space._ [V-CARD-0008]
- comparison sheet — A document showing features of different options. _The comparison sheet shows the difference._ [V-CARD-0009]
- preferred time — The time a customer would like. _Friday at three is your preferred time._ [V-CARD-0010]
- appointment request — A request for an appointment that may still need confirmation. _May I send the appointment request?_ [V-CARD-0011]
- subject to confirmation — Dependent on receiving confirmation before it is definite. _The meeting point is subject to appointment confirmation._ [V-CARD-0012]

## 4. Guided practice
**GP1 (choose-response)** Choose the supported comparison.
  - The sheet states only that the larger version has more boot space. (Both versions have the same boot space. / The larger version has more boot space.)
**GP2 (rewriting)** Ask permission before sending the request.
  - Rewrite: 'I'll send your appointment request now.'
**GP3 (short-answer)** Explain the booking status.
  - The customer has requested Friday at three. Availability is not checked. Is the appointment confirmed?

## 5. Role practice
Use the comparison sheet fact on the cards. Discuss the preferred version. Ask permission to send the request and wait for an answer. Clarify the requested time, proposed meeting point and next contact. Do not act out the test drive.
- **Car Sales Executive**: Agree the details of an appointment request without promising availability. The sheet shows more boot space in the larger version. No appointment slot is confirmed. Ask before sending a request or arranging a confirmation email.
- **Kunal, customer**: Request a test drive in the larger version and understand the next step. Friday at three is your preference. Showroom reception would suit you as a meeting point. You want an email after availability is checked. Answer each permission question.
