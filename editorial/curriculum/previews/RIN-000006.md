## 1. Context
A telephone follow-up after Aditi's showroom visit. She received a quotation for her chosen version and agreed to a follow-up call. Her purchase decision remains open.
- You are: Car Sales Executive following up respectfully on an earlier enquiry.
- People: Aditi (Customer, external)
- Goal: Refer to the earlier quotation, check whether the customer can talk, invite questions and agree the next contact without pressure.
- Note: Do not assume the customer has decided to buy.
- Note: Do not invent prices, stock availability, delivery dates, finance terms or approvals.
- Note: Respect the customer's stated contact preference; give no financial or regulatory advice.

## 2. Notice
**You:** Hello, Aditi. I'm following up on the quotation after your showroom visit. Is now a good time?  _CFN-0022_
**Aditi:** I have a few minutes, but I haven't decided yet.
**You:** Have you had a chance to look at the quotation for your chosen version?  _CFN-0022_
**Aditi:** Yes. I still have a question about the optional additions.
**You:** Which part would you like us to clarify before you consider the quotation further?  _CFN-0022_
**Aditi:** I'd like to know which additions are included. Please reply by email.
**You:** I'll check that question. When would you prefer us to follow up again?  _CFN-0022_
**Aditi:** Please wait until I've read the email. I'll contact you if I need anything else.
**You:** Understood. I'll send the clarification after checking it and leave the next contact to you.  _CFN-0022_
**Aditi:** Thank you. That would help.

## 3. Understand
- **I'm following up on the quotation after your showroom visit.** (CFN-0022) Name the earlier exchange.
- **Have you had a chance to look at the quotation for your chosen version?** (CFN-0022) Invite a response without assuming a decision.
- **When would you prefer us to follow up again?** (CFN-0022) Let the customer state a contact preference.
- following up on — Contacting someone again about an earlier matter. _I'm following up on the quotation._ [V-0313]
- showroom visit — A visit to see and discuss vehicles at a dealership. _I'm calling after your showroom visit._ [V-CARD-0013]
- clarification — An explanation that makes something clearer. _I'll email the clarification after checking it._ [V-CARD-0014]
- purchase decision — A choice about whether or what to buy. _Your purchase decision is still open._ [V-CARD-0015]
- contact preference — How or when someone wants to be contacted. _I'll respect your contact preference._ [V-CARD-0016]
- consider — To think about an option before deciding. _Take time to consider the quotation._ [V-CARD-0017]
- enquiry — A request for information. _I'm following up on your vehicle enquiry._ [V-CARD-0018]

## 4. Guided practice
**GP1 (choose-response)** Choose a respectful opening.
  - You are calling after an agreed showroom follow-up. (I'm following up on your quotation. Is now a good time? / You haven't bought the car yet. Why not?)
**GP2 (rewriting)** Remove the assumed purchase decision.
  - Rewrite: 'Since you've chosen to buy, I'll arrange the next step.' The customer has not decided.
**GP3 (short-answer)** Respect the customer's contact preference.
  - The customer asks for one clarification email and says they will contact you afterwards. What will you do?

## 5. Role practice
Work in pairs. Refer to the earlier showroom visit, check whether the customer can talk, and ask about the quotation. Listen to the contact preference and close accordingly. Change roles.
- **Car Sales Executive**: Find out what the customer needs after the visit. The quotation concerns the chosen version. You cannot assume a purchase decision or answer an unverified inclusion question. Agree the next contact respectfully.
- **Aditi, customer**: Get clarification without committing to buy. You can talk briefly. Ask about optional additions. Request an email, then explain that you will contact the dealership if you need anything else.

## 6. Performance task

- Ask about the customer's preferred next contact.
- Use courteous B1 English without pressure or unsupported claims.
