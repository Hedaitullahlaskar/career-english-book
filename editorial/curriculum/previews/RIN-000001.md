## 1. Context
Your vegetable stall at a local market. Today's tomatoes are ₹50 a kilo. You can offer ₹45 for one kilo.
- You are: Vegetable Seller; you own and run the stall alone.
- People: Rupa (Customer, external); Neel (Regular customer, external)
- Goal: State a price, politely refuse a low offer, and agree a price for one kilo of tomatoes.
- Note: Use only the prices and stock given in each practice.
- Note: Agree only an offer you can make at your own stall.
- Note: Make no health, pesticide or certification claims.

## 2. Notice
**Rupa:** How much are the tomatoes?
**You:** ₹50 a kilo.  _CFN-0085_
**Rupa:** Can you make it ₹40?
**You:** Sorry, I cannot do that.  _CFN-0020_
**Rupa:** What is your best price?
**You:** I can offer ₹45.  _CFN-0087, CFN-0085_
**Rupa:** ₹45 for one kilo?
**You:** Yes, one kilo for ₹45.  _CFN-0085_
**Rupa:** I'll take one kilo, please.
**You:** One kilo for ₹45.  _CFN-0085_

## 3. Understand
- **₹50 a kilo.** (CFN-0085) Say the price and the amount.
- **Sorry, I cannot do that.** (CFN-0020) Politely refuse the customer's offer.
- **I can offer ₹45.** (CFN-0087) Give a price you can accept.
- tomato — A red vegetable sold at the stall. _These tomatoes are ₹50 a kilo._ [V-VEGM-0001]
- price — The money you ask for an item. _The price is ₹50 a kilo._ [V-VEGM-0002]
- cheaper — Costing less money. _₹45 is cheaper than ₹50._ [V-VEGM-0003]
- offer — A price someone suggests. _I cannot accept your ₹40 offer._ [V-VEGM-0004]
- best price — The lowest price you can offer here. _My best price is ₹45 a kilo._ [V-VEGM-0005]

## 4. Guided practice
**GP1 (choose-response)** Choose the seller's reply.
  - The customer asks the price. Tomatoes are ₹50 a kilo. (₹50 a kilo. / Two tomatoes, please. / Here is your change.)
**GP2 (gap-fill)** Complete the polite refusal.
  - Sorry, I ___ do that.
**GP3 (short-answer)** Give a new offer.
  - The customer offers ₹40. Your lowest price is ₹45 a kilo. Offer that price.

## 5. Role practice
Work in pairs. Read your own card. Ask, offer and reply. Agree a price. Then change roles.
- **Vegetable Seller**: Sell one kilo of tomatoes at a price you can accept. Start at ₹50 a kilo. You can accept ₹45, but not ₹40.
- **Rupa, customer**: Buy one kilo of tomatoes. Offer ₹40 first. Ask for the best price. You can accept ₹45.

## 6. Performance task
Neel wants one kilo of tomatoes. In this new practice, your price is ₹60 a kilo and your lowest price is ₹55. Neel offers ₹50, then accepts ₹55. Speak as the seller without reading the model.
- State ₹60 a kilo.
- Politely refuse ₹50.
- Offer ₹55.
- Confirm one kilo for ₹55.
